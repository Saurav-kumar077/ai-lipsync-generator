UNIVO Education Pvt. Ltd. is an Indian online higher education company that partners with universities and educational institutions to deliver scalable, high-quality online degree programs and learning experiences. Founded with the mission to make education more accessible, flexible, and affordable, UNIVO supports institutions with technology, program development, and student success services so they can reach learners across India and the world. The company also focuses on innovation in online learning, student engagement, and expanding access to education, helping bridge the gap between traditional campuses and modern digital learning needs.
